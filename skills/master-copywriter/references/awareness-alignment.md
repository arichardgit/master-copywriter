# Awareness Level Alignment Protocol

Carried over from Alex's earlier copywriting skill. This is the enforcement layer on top of Schwartz. Schwartz tells you which awareness level to write to. This tells you not to drift off it halfway through.

**One ad = one avatar = one awareness level = one continuous conversation.**

## The critical rule: hook and body must target the same avatar

Before writing any ad copy:

1. Identify the single awareness level you are targeting.
2. Make sure the hook and the body speak to the same person at the same stage.
3. Never switch avatars mid-ad.

## The five awareness levels

- **Stage 1, Unaware.** Doesn't know they have a problem.
- **Stage 2, Problem Aware.** Knows the problem, doesn't know solutions exist.
- **Stage 3, Solution Aware.** Knows the solution category, doesn't know your product.
- **Stage 4, Product Aware.** Knows your product exists, doesn't know why it's different.
- **Stage 5, Most Aware.** Already a customer or ready to buy, comparing final details.

## Mandatory check before finalizing any ad

**1. Who does my HOOK call out?** What is this person thinking about right now? What awareness stage are they at? What problem or desire made them stop scrolling?

**2. Who does my BODY speak to?** What knowledge does this person already have? What awareness stage does the body assume? What objections does the body answer?

**3. Are these the same person?** If no, you have a mismatch. Rewrite either the hook or the body. If yes, proceed.

## Three fatal mismatches

**Problem Aware hook plus Product Aware body.**
Hook: "Tired of acne that won't go away?"
Body: "Our retinol serum has 2.5% concentration with time-release technology..."
Why it fails: the person who stopped doesn't know retinol is the solution yet. You skipped stages.

**Solution or Product Aware hook plus Most Aware body.**
Hook: "This castor oil finally smoothed out the texture on my face."
Body: "For all the people obsessed with what bottle your castor oil is in. This one is amber, hexane-free, refillable glass..."
Why it fails: the person discovering castor oil for texture does not care about hexane or bottles yet. You switched to talking to an expert.

**Most Aware hook plus Problem Aware body.**
Hook: "Why our BlendTech 3000 beats every Vitamix model..."
Body: "Sick of lumpy smoothies? Here's why blending matters..."
Why it fails: the person comparing BlendTech to Vitamix already knows blenders matter. Do not educate backwards.

## The fix protocol

When you detect a mismatch you have two options, and only two.

**Option A: keep the hook, rewrite the body.** Match the body to whoever you called out in the hook. Continue the conversation at their level.

**Option B: keep the body, rewrite the hook.** Change the hook to call out whoever the body is speaking to.

## Enforcement checklist

Before submitting any ad script:

- I can clearly name the ONE avatar this ad targets.
- I can identify what awareness stage they are at.
- My hook calls out a person at Stage ___.
- My body speaks to a person at Stage ___.
- These stages match, or are adjacent.
- The entire ad follows one continuous conversation with one person.

If you cannot check every box, the ad will fail. Rewrite before proceeding.

## What correct alignment looks like

**Product Aware to Product Aware.**
Hook: "This castor oil is different from any bottle you've tried..."
Body: "Most castor oils use hexane extraction that strips the nutrients. Ours is cold-pressed and stored in amber glass so the ricinoleic acid stays potent. That's why it actually absorbs into your skin instead of sitting on top..."
Both speak to someone who knows castor oil exists and wants a better version.

**Solution Aware to Solution Aware.**
Hook: "The real reason your smoothies taste like grass..."
Body: "It's not your ingredients. It's that cheap blenders don't break down the cell walls in leafy greens. A high-powered blender like ours has 1200 watts that pulverizes everything in 30 seconds, so you get creamy texture and your body can actually absorb the nutrients..."
Both speak to someone who makes smoothies but doesn't know equipment quality matters yet.

**Most Aware to Most Aware.**
Hook: "If you're buying castor oil in plastic bottles, you're wasting money..."
Body: "Plastic leaches into the oil and breaks down the nutrients. Our amber glass blocks UV light and keeps it fresh for 2 years. Plus it's hexane-free and comes with free shipping..."
Both speak to an existing castor oil user comparing quality details.

## Final reminder

Never make the customer do mental gymnastics to follow you.

If you hook a beginner, finish talking to a beginner. If you hook an expert, finish talking to an expert.

Mismatched awareness means a confused customer, bad metrics, and wasted money. When in doubt, stay at one level throughout the entire ad.
